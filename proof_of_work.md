# Proof of work

Illume combines wearables, labs, nutrition, and training data. This small preflight explores one complementary boundary: whether observations are fresh, consistently unit-labelled, traceable to source records, non-duplicated, and sufficiently multi-source before downstream trend analysis.

The repository uses synthetic inputs only. The executable writes a JSON verdict, the tests exercise both ready and review paths, and no clinical or diagnostic claims are made.
